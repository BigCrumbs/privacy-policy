Privacy Policy for Screen Tariff

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Screen Tariff ("the App") is developed and operated by Big Crumbs LLC. This privacy policy explains how the App handles information when you use it on iOS and Android.

Screen Tariff is a self-regulation productivity application that helps users manage their screen time by introducing a voluntary ad break before accessing selected apps. The App is designed with privacy in mind: we collect no personal information and require no account creation.

Information We Collect

Screen Tariff does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library

Locally Stored Data

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your sanction settings (which apps you have sanctioned, ad break lengths, unlock times, access modes, and relock warning preferences)
  • Active session state (timer countdowns and unlock states)
  • Activity used for Insights, such as the number of sessions, time unlocked, and ad breaks you cancelled, which the App uses to build your weekly summaries

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. It exists solely to provide the App's core functionality. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Screen Time Access (iOS)

On iOS, Screen Tariff uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to block the apps you choose and to unlock them for the time you set. When you select apps to sanction, iOS gives Screen Tariff private tokens that stand in for those apps; the App does not receive a list of the apps installed on your device, and it cannot see your activity inside other apps. All Screen Time processing happens on your device, and this data is never transmitted externally.

Usage Access Permission (Android)

On Android, Screen Tariff may request access to your device's usage statistics to enable OS-level app enforcement features. This data is used solely to detect when a sanctioned app is opened and to enforce the ad break. This information is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted externally.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App uses them to send relock warnings and weekly recaps. These notifications are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us.

Advertising

Screen Tariff is free and supported by ads. Some ads are served by Google AdMob. As part of serving ads, Google may collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you have allowed tracking)
  • IP address
  • General location information derived from IP address
  • Information about your interactions with ads

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy, which can be reviewed at policies.google.com/privacy. You can limit personalized advertising in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or tracking for Screen Tariff
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, and delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization

Screen Tariff does not have access to any data collected by Google AdMob.

Some ads are custom ads that we show directly within the App on behalf of advertisers. Custom ads do not collect or share any information about you. If you tap one, it opens the advertiser's website in your browser, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children's Privacy

Screen Tariff is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
