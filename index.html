Privacy Policy for Screen Tariff

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Screen Tariff ("the App") is developed and operated by Big Crumbs LLC. This privacy policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Screen Tariff is a self-regulation productivity application that helps users manage their screen time by introducing a voluntary buffer period before accessing selected apps. The App is designed with privacy in mind — we collect no personal information and require no account creation.

Information We Collect

Screen Tariff does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library

Locally Stored Data

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your sanction settings (which apps you have sanctioned, buffer durations, and access modes)
  • Active session state (timer countdowns and unlock states)

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. It exists solely to provide the App's core functionality.

Advertising

Screen Tariff displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. As part of serving ads, Google may collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location information derived from IP address
  • Information about your interactions with ads

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy, which can be reviewed at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's advertising settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Screen Tariff does not have access to any data collected by Google AdMob.

Children's Privacy

Screen Tariff is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Usage Access Permission (Android)

Screen Tariff may request access to your device's usage statistics to enable OS-level app enforcement features. This data is used solely to detect when a sanctioned app is opened and to enforce the buffer period. This information is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted externally.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
